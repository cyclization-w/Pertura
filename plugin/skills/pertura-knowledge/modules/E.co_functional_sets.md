# E.co_functional_sets: Regulators of one program that act together

**The biology.** The regulators of one program, clustered by what else they move into sets that act together. Some
published screens cluster regulators into co-functional sets (P1.5). This module clusters them by the correlation of
their effects on the other programs (Pertura's choice).

**The measurement.** The regulators that move the program at q < 0.05, clustered by the correlation of their effects
on the other programs. Per set, its members and mean effects.

## Ways the result can mislead

### the number of sets is a cut of the tree

- **Shows in:** `sets`; the result table's `silhouette` (the cut chosen by the silhouette)
- **Read:** PG6
- **Do:** stating the cut helps; other cuts are a choice worth trying.

### a set of a few weak regulators can reflect noise

- **Shows in:** each set's members' effects on the program (the table `members`)
- **Read:** PF16, ES1
- **Do:** the members' effects and cells show how much a set rests on.
