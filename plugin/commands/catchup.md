description: Catch up on a Pertura analysis after being away - the decisions waiting on you, the agent's thread and its reason, the changes since, and the options next
---

First load the Pertura skill with the Skill tool (`pertura:pertura`) if this session has not loaded it. Then call
`pertura_status` and give the user its digest, in their language and in plain words, in this order:
- the decisions waiting on them (the design, the results to confirm, the end, a costly step), or none;
- the thread you are on, and your reason for it;
- the changes since they last looked, by thread;
- the open threads that may come next, as options with their evidence.

Then go on with your work, unless their reply redirects it.
